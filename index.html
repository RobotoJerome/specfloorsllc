What We Install

Hard-surface flooring, done to spec.

Spec Floors is a hard-surface specialist. That focus is the point — these are the materials Nick installs every week, so the cuts, transitions and seams come out clean without a learning curve on your job.

Hardwood

Solid hardwood installed flat, tight and square — nail-down or glue-down, with clean racking so the grain reads naturally across the room.

Engineered Wood

Real wood over a stable core — a smart pick for basements, slabs and radiant heat where solid hardwood can't go.

Laminate

Durable, budget-friendly and tough on scratches. Great for busy households, kids and dogs — installed with proper expansion gaps so it stays flat.

Most Requested

LVP & EVP

Luxury and enhanced vinyl plank — waterproof, warm underfoot and nearly indestructible. The go-to for kitchens, baths, basements and full-home remodels.

Rubber Tile

Gyms, weight rooms, locker rooms and shops. Seams butted tight and rolled flat so edges don't curl under heavy traffic and equipment.

VCT & Carpet Tile

Commercial-grade vinyl composition tile and carpet tile for schools, offices, retail and medical — laid on a true grid, no drifting rows.

Residential Your home, treated like one.

Whole-house remodels, single rooms, kitchens, basements, stairs and landings. Furniture moved, old flooring pulled, subfloor checked and prepped, new floor laid, transitions and trim finished. Cleaned up at the end of every day — not just at the end of the job.

  • Tear-out and haul-away of old flooring
  • Subfloor prep, leveling and moisture checks
  • Stairs, treads, risers and landings
  • Trim, transitions and flush vent cut-ins

Commercial On schedule, on spec.

Offices, schools and daycares, medical suites, retail, gyms, common areas and elevator cabs. Nick works around your hours — nights and weekends when the space has to stay open — and leaves a jobsite your tenants can walk into.

  • After-hours and weekend scheduling
  • Phased installs to keep spaces in service
  • Rubber tile, VCT, carpet tile and commercial LVP
  • Fully insured — COI available on request

Meet Nick

You're not just hiring a flooring contractor.
You're letting someone into your home.

Nick Herting has been installing floors for more than ten years — hardwood, laminate, vinyl plank, rubber and VCT — in everything from older McHenry County homes to busy commercial buildings. Along the way he figured out something a lot of contractors never do: the floor is only half the job. The other half is what it's like to have someone working in your house.

So Nick shows up when he says he will. He tells you what he's doing and why, in plain English, before he starts — and if he finds something under the old floor you should know about, you hear it from him that day, not on the invoice. He keeps the work area picked up, keeps the dust down, and when he pulls out at the end of the day, your home is still a home.

Then there's the part you'll be looking at for the next twenty years: the details. Seams that line up. Transitions that sit flat. Vents cut in flush with the grain. Stair treads and risers that look like they grew there. Nick's standard is simple — if it wouldn't pass in his own house, it doesn't get left in yours.

He's a lifelong McHenry County resident, and he still lives here. Your neighbors are his neighbors, and his next job usually comes from the last one. That's not a marketing line — it's just the reason he can't afford to cut a corner, and the reason he wouldn't want to anyway.
